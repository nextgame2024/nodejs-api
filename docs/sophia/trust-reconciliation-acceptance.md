# TRUST receipt reconciliation acceptance test

Pedro can run this in the test workspace after the matching Sophia backend and frontend are deployed. Use a connected Xero test organisation assigned the TRUST role, a synthetic authorised AUD invoice for 2,741.80 and a corresponding test deposit for 2,742.00. Record the invoice number, payment ID and import time so a failed check can be reproduced.

| Step | What to do | What to expect in Sophia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Verify the deposit | In **Payments and Control**, open the outstanding TRUST invoice. Choose **Verify a TRUST bank deposit**, enter the bank date, account, reference, transaction ID and full amount. Optionally attach a test screenshot. | One verified receipt shows AUD 2,742.00 and AUD 0.20 above the invoice balance. The receipt appears under **Review unlinked receipts**. |
| 2. Link the invoice payment | Once Xero has the test invoice payment for AUD 2,741.80, choose TRUST under **Organisation to refresh** in Payment operations and select **Refresh**. Wait for the import to finish. The TRUST queue updates on the current page. Open the receipt, choose **Refresh imported details**, compare the suggested ID with Xero and confirm the link. | The receipt shows the linked payment ID and AUD 2,741.80. The remaining AUD 0.20 appears under **Review excess receipts**. If a corresponding overpayment exists in Xero, review and link it separately. |
| 3. Check the handoff | Open **Review bank reconciliation**. Search for the payment ID. Open the linked Xero payment and compare the bank date and amount. Use the existing payment when reconciling the test statement in Xero. | While the imported payment is pending, the review queue shows AUD 2,741.80. Pedro can claim it. A manual check entered after seeing reconciliation in Xero records who checked it and when. |
| 4. Confirm the outcome | After a TRUST import includes the reconciled flag, search in **Payments and Control** under **View reconciled payments**. | One confirmed invoice payment totals AUD 2,741.80. That payment leaves the review queue. Its receipt, check and linked overpayment are still available on the invoice. |
| 5. Check exceptions | Using test fixtures, import a later pending flag, an omitted payment list, or conflicting flags. Open the same invoice and payment ID. | A pending flag returns the payment to review. Missing or conflicting flags show an unavailable status. The historical receipt and manual check stay visible, and the confirmed total excludes that payment. |

**Verify a deposit from a statement CSV.** In the receipt form, enter the receiving TRUST account name and open **Use a bank statement CSV**. Download the header and use synthetic rows in that format. Include two identical booked rows with the same transaction ID, a third booked row for the same amount with a different ID, a pending receipt and a PTY receipt. Expect two selectable TRUST deposits and counts explaining the other rows. Add a conflicting amount for the first ID and check that both versions are withheld. Select the other ID and choose **Use selected row in receipt form**. Compare the date, AUD amount, reference and ID before saving. If Xero already records the payment, select it in the form. After verifying, reopen the same statement and confirm that the saved transaction is excluded for this invoice. The CSV is previewed locally. The saved receipt records the reviewed fields, CSV filename, SHA-256 fingerprint, record number and any separately supplied screenshot. The manager keeps the original CSV.

**Save a deposit before identifying its invoice.** Under TRUST in **Payments and Control**, open **Deposits awaiting an invoice**, enter the account, and preview the synthetic CSV. Select a row, choose **Review deposit for staging**, then **Save deposit without an invoice**. Reopen the queue, search by transaction ID and confirm that overlapping exports of the same movement yield one saved deposit. Once the invoice is known, open its TRUST receipt form, choose **Choose a saved deposit**, select the movement and use **Replace receipt draft with this deposit**. Compare the amount and bank evidence, select the existing Xero payment if applicable, and explicitly verify. The receipt should retain its CSV record and the matched movement should leave the queue. A second same-day deposit with a different bank ID should remain.

**Find an invoice for a staged deposit.** Select the deposit in **Deposits awaiting an invoice**, then choose **Find possible invoices**. With synthetic fixtures, compare an exact invoice number, a number embedded in a bank description, an exact invoice reference, and two invoices with the same balance. Expect the matching reason, the AUD excess or remaining balance, and the number of invoices sharing the same evidence. An amount-only match requires checking the payer. A deposit with no matching reference or amount has no suggestions. Use the manual search to look up a contact, invoice number or reference. Open the invoice with **Review**, check the bank evidence, and choose **Use reviewed deposit** only when satisfied. Opening a suggestion leaves the invoice balance and the staged deposit unchanged.

**Preview a combined receipt.** Stage a synthetic AUD 3,570 deposit. In **Find possible invoices**, search for an open AUD 3,500 invoice, choose **Add to split** and enter 3,000. Search for a second invoice owing AUD 568, add it and enter 568. Choose **Check split against imported balances**. Expect AUD 500 due on the first invoice, zero due on the second, AUD 3,568 allocated and AUD 2 unassigned. Edit an amount and confirm the previous results disappear. Try allocating more than the second invoice balance. After an import changes the first balance, check the old draft again and confirm Sophia requests a fresh review. Remove the changed invoice and add it again from a fresh search to use the new balance. Reopening the deposit starts a fresh draft. This preview does not reserve funds, verify a receipt or post to Xero.

**Review an invoice match.** On a synthetic staged deposit, choose **Find possible invoices**, then **Record match decision**. Enter what identifies the payer and accept the invoice. Confirm that the deposit is still awaiting verification. Accept a different eligible invoice and check that the first acceptance shows as superseded. Dismiss the second invoice with a correction reason. In **Match decision history**, check both reasons, the reviewer, the order and the matching evidence. With two tabs open, submit from the older view after a new decision and confirm it requests a fresh review. Verify the deposit on the correct invoice, then open the history from the saved receipt.

**Measure match outcomes.** In the TRUST panel, open **Match outcomes** and set the bank deposit dates to cover the synthetic test statements. Stage three deposits. Accept invoice A for the first two, verify one on A and the other on B, and leave the third deposit awaiting review. Expect three deposits, two reviewed and agreement of one out of two (50%). Select **Verified on another invoice** to see the two invoice numbers and open the recorded decision history. The outcome filter narrows the deposit list; the summary and score breakdown continue to cover the selected dates. The median measures elapsed minutes from staging to the first recorded decision. A dismissed or superseded acceptance cannot count as a current match. Conflicting bank evidence is counted separately. This report measures agreement with Sophia receipt verification; independent accountant validation is a separate pilot check.

**Check the saved statement source.** Select a CSV deposit and check that its account, date, amount, reference and transaction ID are locked. Use **Detach source to edit bank details** to turn it into a manual entry, or select the original row again and verify it. Open **Statement source** on the saved receipt and choose a renamed copy of the original file. Expect a file and deposit match. Add a trailing newline to a copy and check that Sophia reports different contents. Cancel a comparison or select another file and confirm that the saved receipt is unchanged. Older receipts without source metadata continue to show their original evidence.

**When the feed arrives later.** Monday verification can be completed from the bank evidence. Resume at step 2 when the invoice payment is available in the Xero import. **Refresh** requests an import for the selected organisation. **Check progress** reads the status of an active manual import. The receipt's **Refresh imported details** reloads data already stored in Sophia. If the import supplies no reconciliation flag, record the manual check and leave automated confirmation pending.

**When Xero has already recorded the payment.** In Sophia’s **Payments and Control**, choose **Review payments without receipt links** under TRUST. Search by the exact payment ID or part of the Xero payment reference. Apply the Xero payment date and AUD 2,741.80 as the payment amount. Check the invoice row shows one available payment for that amount. Open the paid test invoice, expand **Verify a TRUST bank deposit** and select the imported payment for AUD 2,741.80. Enter the date and full AUD 2,742.00 from the bank evidence. Choose **Verify receipt and link existing payment**. Check that the linked payment leaves the queue. Check for one verified deposit, one linked invoice payment, AUD 0.00 invoice balance at verification and AUD 0.20 in **Review excess receipts**. Retry the same submission after a simulated lost response and check that the counts stay at one. For a partially paid invoice, use a bank deposit equal to the selected payment. If a Monday receipt already exists, open that verification to link the payment.

**Queue checks.** Find the unlinked receipt by part of its bank reference. After linking the excess, search **Review excess receipts** by its full Xero overpayment ID. Choose 50 documents per page and jump to a later page in a larger test dataset. Completing a refresh should preserve the queue, filters and page when that page still exists.

**Invoice exception test.** Using separate test fixtures, verify a receipt and then import an invoice marked voided or deleted, or complete a full import that omits it. Open **Review invoice exceptions** and search for the receipt reference, bank transaction ID, or linked Xero payment or overpayment ID. Check the reason, number of receipts, verified AUD total and latest receipt date. Open the invoice to view its evidence and historical links. New deposit verification should be unavailable. If a subsequent import restores the authorised AUD invoice with a positive balance, refresh the details and check that verification is available again.

**Pass criteria.** The receipt, invoice payment and excess retain their respective amounts. Retrying a save or link preserves the original record. A manual check alone leaves the payment outside the confirmed list. Capture any failing step, last successful import time, payment ID and expected versus displayed amount in the test report.
