## MVP and release boundaries

The first usable increment gives authorised staff a read-only operational overview across TRUST and PTY. The **MVP ends at Phase 2** when staff can also confirm identity, verify a bank receipt, allocate part of an authorised AUD invoice, post once with approval, and hand off final reconciliation to Xero. Users can open a case for every other scenario from the first operational release. Unavailable sources show “awaiting data” with an owner and next action. Historic imported payments and unverified projections cannot become executable work merely by opening the register.

### Scope of exceptional cases

| Case | MVP behaviour | Later resolution |
|---|---|---|
| One receipt covers multiple invoices / several receipts cover one invoice | Operator may propose explicit combined splits and complete in Xero. Support successive approved receipts on one invoice with current remaining balance. | Phase 4 approved eligible Xero batches and per-member read-back. |
| Same amount, parent/sponsor, wrong/missing ref, unidentified receipt | Show alternatives, confirmed relationship and conflicts. Allow manual lookup, hold and assign investigation; save decision evidence. | Expand reviewed payer relationships and measure recall. |
| Duplicate bank/Stripe events, manual Xero posting, historic imports | Bank replay and near-duplicate controls, existing payment lookup, history tagged imported. Unsupported processor events stay evidence-only. Historic pairs need a confirmed link. | Phase 3 enforces merchant/mode/object uniqueness and backfills historic processor members. |
| Refund, reversal, chargeback or partial college refund | Flag contradictory or reversed evidence, freeze affected action, reopen obligation for review, preserve original. Accounting corrections via Xero with recorded reason. | Phases 3–4 ingest disputes/partial recoveries, post approved reversals or record actual refunds, recalculate residual. |
| FX and invoice edits after application | Preserve original currency and dates. Block mismatched or amended proposals, invalidate stale approval, show both balances and retain previous intent. | Phase 4 adds approved rate source/date, bank vs invoice currency and separate rounding/gain or loss treatment following accountant validation. |
| Revoked connection or TRUST success / PTY failure | Degrade only affected company view, pause outstanding writes, show leg-specific receipts. An already successful TRUST write survives. | Independent replay after repair of the failed PTY action. Same rule applies to mail/attachment failures. |
| New commission rate or course variation | Surface calculation version, original invoice and discrepancy. Hold affected claim, preserve prior receipts. | Phase 3 versions entitlements and records approved amendments or credit note with its own receipt. |
| Overpaid student, future invoice, unclaimed credit | Detect exact excess before posting, create student residual, age it, route to reviewed Xero treatment. | Phase 4 approved carry-forward to eligible same-contact invoice, refund or accountant-authorised adjustment. |
| Direct payment with no TRUST invoice, Stripe paid before payout | Capture evidence/task, no automatic posting, distinguish source unavailable and not applicable. | Phase 3 completes external confirmation, commission origin, processor-to-bank matching and due-date alerts. |

## Test and operational validation

Use synthetic names, fake merchant/account IDs and fixed business dates. Freeze time around approval expiry, Xero's idempotency window, Brisbane midnight, bank value date and Stripe expected arrival. Unit-test scoring/decimal maths and state transitions. Run PostgreSQL integration tests using the runtime role and RLS, Nest connector contracts against recorded **synthetic** responses, and Angular tests through normal manager navigation. Run fault-injection between provider success and local receipt commit. Financial tests pass only when both the expected actions and the absence of unintended accounting movements can be asserted.

**Demonstration and rollout.** After design approval, use two separately authorised disposable Xero organisations with labelled roles, fake statements and Stripe test mode. Manually reconcile a synthetic receipt in the Xero UI and record before/after API observations. Verify actual schema, documentation discrepancies and account-specific restrictions there. Use a fake mail sink and signed callback fixtures; the Xero demo organisation cannot exercise invoice email delivery. No production migrations, Xero writes, Stripe movements or external emails are part of this review or the automated tests.

Start any subsequently approved pilot read-only. Finance compares each entity's open invoices, credit balances and selected payments against same-date Xero records with an agreed sample. Run matching in suggestion-only mode, record cases with missing evidence, time-to-review and precision by score band. Enable only the agreed tenant/operation after the sample ties and concurrency/unknown-outcome tests pass. Monitor ambiguous writes, stale connections, unmatched residuals, overdue colleges, credit ageing and duplicate postings. A kill switch stops new dispatch while allowing read-only recovery of in-flight operations. Close the pilot with finance sign-off on discrepancies and the decision to expand.
